PS-01: Real-World Asset Tokenization Platform
Organisation Name: EkamVistar 
Background
A plot of land. A tractor. A truck of grain. A ₹40 lakh invoice. Each is worth real money and each belongs to someone. Yet proving who owns it, whether it is genuine and who may buy it still depends on scattered files, manual approvals and records that no two parties fully trust.
Anyone can mint a token that claims to be a building. The real problem is making that claim verifiable, governed and traceable, from the physical world to the ledger and back.
Problem Statement (Internship Opportunity for potential candidates )
Build a platform on Hyperledger Fabric that takes a real-world asset from registration to retirement: verified, valued, digitally represented, and governed by who owns and transfers it, and that works for different kinds of assets, not just one.











Objective
Your system should:
Multiple asset types: At least two substantially different assets (e.g. land and a vehicle) running on the same platform, with different data, steps and ownership. Adding another asset type must not mean redesigning the platform.
Registration & verification: Assets are registered with their details and supporting evidence, and go through a verification and approval process before anything is tokenized.
Valuation: Every asset carries its own valuation, with its method, date and source. The same type does not mean the same value.
Tokenization: A digital representation is created only for approved assets. It must be traceable: from a token ID anyone can identify the real asset behind it. Define what it represents and whether it is whole or fractional.
Ownership & transfer: Ownership is assigned and can be transferred. Every transfer is checked against rules (e.g. identity, eligibility, limits). Rejected transfers are recorded with the reason.
Lifecycle: Each asset moves through defined states up to redemption or retirement. Every change records who did it, when, from which state to which, and why.
Roles & visibility: Different organizations and roles (issuer, verifier, investor, compliance, auditor, administrator) see and do different things.
Audit: The complete history of an asset, token and transfer can be inspected.
